DataSync Solutions - Acquisition Report

Date: September 2021
Acquiring Company: TechVenture Inc.
Acquired Company: DataSync Solutions
Acquisition Price: $8 Million

Executive Summary:
On September 15, 2021, TechVenture Inc. completed the acquisition of DataSync Solutions, a data integration startup based in Austin, Texas. The acquisition was structured as an all-cash deal valued at $8 million.

DataSync Solutions Background:
DataSync Solutions was founded in 2017 by Robert Kim and Lisa Zhang. The company specialized in real-time data synchronization tools for enterprise applications. Prior to the acquisition, DataSync had:
- 45 employees
- Annual revenue of $3.2M
- 120 enterprise customers
- Patents in real-time sync algorithms

Strategic Rationale:
1. Technology Integration: DataSync's real-time sync technology enhances ProjectFlow's capabilities
2. Customer Base Expansion: Access to 120 new enterprise accounts
3. Talent Acquisition: Key engineering talent, including founders Robert Kim and Lisa Zhang
4. Competitive Advantage: Proprietary algorithms for data consistency

Integration Plan:
- Phase 1 (Q4 2021): Complete legal and financial integration
- Phase 2 (Q1 2022): Merge engineering teams
- Phase 3 (Q2 2022): Product integration with ProjectFlow
- Phase 4 (Q3 2022): Unified go-to-market strategy

Post-Acquisition Leadership:
- Robert Kim: VP of Data Products (reports to CTO Michael Rodriguez)
- Lisa Zhang: Director of Engineering, Data Platform

Key Risks Identified:
- Integration complexity with existing systems
- Retention of key DataSync employees
- Customer migration challenges

Synergy Projections:
- Expected cost savings: $1.5M annually by 2023
- Revenue synergies: $5M additional revenue by 2024
