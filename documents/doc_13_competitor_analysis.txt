Competitor Analysis: Enterprise Project Management Market

Prepared by: Strategy Team
Date: August 2024
Classification: Internal Use Only

Market Overview:
The enterprise project management software market is valued at $6.7B globally, with projected growth to $9.8B by 2027 (CAGR: 10.2%).

Key Competitors:

1. Atlassian (Jira)
   - Market Position: Leader
   - Strength: Developer-focused, strong ecosystem
   - Weakness: Complex for non-technical users
   - Pricing: $8.15-16.25/user/month
   - Market Share: 28%

2. Monday.com
   - Market Position: Fast-growing challenger
   - Strength: User-friendly, visual interface
   - Weakness: Limited enterprise features
   - Pricing: $10-20/user/month
   - Market Share: 12%

3. Asana
   - Market Position: Established player
   - Strength: Strong workflow automation
   - Weakness: Expensive at scale
   - Pricing: $13.49-30.49/user/month
   - Market Share: 15%

4. Smartsheet
   - Market Position: Enterprise-focused
   - Strength: Spreadsheet-familiar interface
   - Weakness: Learning curve for advanced features
   - Pricing: $9-25/user/month
   - Market Share: 8%

5. Microsoft Project
   - Market Position: Legacy leader
   - Strength: Microsoft ecosystem integration
   - Weakness: Outdated UX, slow innovation
   - Pricing: $10-55/user/month
   - Market Share: 18%
