Employee Handbook - TechVenture Inc.

Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Version: 4.0

Welcome to TechVenture!

Company Mission:
To empower teams worldwide with intelligent software that transforms how work gets done.

Core Values:
1. Customer Obsession - We put customers first in everything we do
2. Innovation - We embrace new ideas and technologies
3. Transparency - We communicate openly and honestly
4. Collaboration - We achieve more together
5. Excellence - We strive for the highest quality

Employment Policies:

Work Arrangements:
- Hybrid Work: 3 days in office, 2 days remote (standard)
- Fully Remote: Available for approved positions
- Core Hours: 10 AM - 4 PM local time
- Flexible start/end times

Compensation:
- Competitive base salary (reviewed annually)
- Annual bonus: Up to 15% of base salary
- Equity grants for all full-time employees
- 401(k) match: 100% up to 4% of salary

Time Off:
- Paid Time Off: 20 days per year (increases with tenure)
- Sick Leave: Unlimited (trust-based)
- Parental Leave: 16 weeks paid (all parents)
- Holidays: 10 company holidays plus 2 floating holidays
- Sabbatical: 4 weeks after 5 years of service

Benefits:
- Health Insurance: Medical, dental, vision (100% premium covered)
- Life Insurance: 2x annual salary
- Disability Insurance: Short-term and long-term
- Mental Health: Free therapy sessions through Modern Health
- Wellness Stipend: $1,200/year

Professional Development:
- Learning Budget: $2,000/year per employee
- Conference Attendance: Covered for relevant events
- Internal Mentorship Program
- Leadership Development Track

Office Perks:
- Free lunch daily (SF and Austin offices)
- Snacks and beverages
- Gym membership reimbursement
- Commuter benefits

Code of Conduct:
TechVenture is committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive workplace. Discrimination, harassment, and unethical behavior are not tolerated.

Questions?
Contact HR at hr@techventure.com or visit our internal HR portal.
