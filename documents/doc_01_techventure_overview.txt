TechVenture Inc. - Company Overview

Founded: March 15, 2018
Headquarters: San Francisco, California
CEO: Sarah Chen
Industry: Enterprise Software

Company History:
TechVenture Inc. was founded in 2018 by Sarah Chen and Michael Rodriguez after they left positions at major tech companies. The company began with a focus on cloud-based project management tools and has since expanded into enterprise AI solutions.

Key Milestones:
- 2018: Company founded with $2M seed funding
- 2019: Launched ProjectFlow, their flagship project management platform
- 2020: Raised Series A funding of $15M led by Horizon Ventures
- 2021: Acquired DataSync Solutions for $8M
- 2022: Reached 500,000 paying users
- 2023: Launched AI-powered analytics suite "InsightEngine"
- 2024: Expanded to European markets, opened London office

Products:
1. ProjectFlow - Cloud-based project management (launched 2019)
2. TeamConnect - Internal communication platform (launched 2020)
3. DataSync - Data integration tools (acquired 2021)
4. InsightEngine - AI analytics (launched 2023)

Financial Information:
- 2022 Revenue: $45M
- 2023 Revenue: $78M
- 2024 Revenue: $125M (projected)

Employee Count:
- 2020: 85 employees
- 2022: 210 employees
- 2024: 450 employees

Board of Directors:
- Sarah Chen (CEO, Co-founder)
- Michael Rodriguez (CTO, Co-founder)
- Jennifer Walsh (Horizon Ventures)
