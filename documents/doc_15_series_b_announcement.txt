Press Release - TechVenture Announces Series B Funding

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Date: March 15, 2024
Contact: Media Relations, TechVenture Inc.

TechVenture Inc. Raises $75M Series B to Accelerate AI-Powered Enterprise Software

SAN FRANCISCO, CA - TechVenture Inc., the innovative enterprise software company, today announced the closing of a $75 million Series B funding round. The round was led by Summit Partners, with participation from existing investor Horizon Ventures and new investors GV (Google Ventures) and Accel.

Funding Details:
- Round Size: $75 million
- Lead Investor: Summit Partners
- Participating Investors: Horizon Ventures, GV, Accel
- Post-Money Valuation: $500 million
- Total Funding to Date: $92 million

Use of Funds:
The new capital will be used to:
1. Accelerate AI product development, particularly InsightEngine
2. Expand European operations (doubling UK team)
3. Enter Asia Pacific market (Singapore office planned)
4. Grow engineering team by 100 people
5. Strategic acquisitions in AI/ML space

Key Metrics Supporting the Raise:
- Annual Recurring Revenue: $100M+ (as of Q1 2024)
- Year-over-Year Revenue Growth: 75%
- Net Revenue Retention: 125%
- Enterprise Customers: 1,500+
- Total Users: 750,000+

Executive Quotes:

"This funding validates our vision for AI-powered enterprise software. We've proven product-market fit with InsightEngine and are now ready to scale globally." - Sarah Chen, CEO

"TechVenture is building the future of how teams work. Their AI capabilities are truly differentiated, and we're excited to support their growth." - Tom Bradley, Managing Director, Summit Partners

New Board Member:
Tom Bradley from Summit Partners will join TechVenture's Board of Directors.

About TechVenture Inc:
TechVenture provides AI-powered enterprise software for project management, analytics, and team collaboration. Founded in 2018, the company serves over 750,000 users at 1,500+ enterprises worldwide.
