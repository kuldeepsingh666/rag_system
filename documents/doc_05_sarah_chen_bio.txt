Sarah Chen - Executive Biography

Name: Sarah Chen
Title: Chief Executive Officer & Co-Founder
Company: TechVenture Inc.

Professional Background:
Sarah Chen is the CEO and co-founder of TechVenture Inc., a position she has held since founding the company in March 2018. Under her leadership, TechVenture has grown from a two-person startup to a 450+ employee company with over $125M in annual revenue.

Prior Experience:
Before founding TechVenture, Sarah held senior positions at major technology companies:

- 2014-2018: Senior Director of Product, CloudSoft Inc.
  - Led a team of 40 product managers
  - Launched three successful enterprise products
  - Grew division revenue from $50M to $180M

- 2010-2014: Product Manager, TechGiant Corp
  - Managed flagship productivity suite
  - Oversaw integration of acquired products
  - Received "Innovator of the Year" award in 2013

- 2007-2010: Software Engineer, StartupXYZ
  - Early engineer at successful startup (acquired by TechGiant)

Education:
- MBA, Stanford Graduate School of Business, 2010
- BS Computer Science, MIT, 2007

Board Memberships:
- TechVenture Inc. (CEO, Co-founder)
- Bay Area Technology Council (Board Member)
- Women in Tech Foundation (Advisory Board)

Awards and Recognition:
- Forbes 40 Under 40, 2022
- Inc. Magazine Top 100 Female Founders, 2023
- Bay Area Business Journal CEO of the Year Finalist, 2023

Personal:
Sarah resides in San Francisco with her family. She is an advocate for diversity in technology and mentors aspiring entrepreneurs through the Stanford Alumni Mentorship Program.
