Internal Memo: Product Launch Date Update

From: Sarah Chen, CEO
To: All Employees
Date: December 20, 2022
Subject: InsightEngine Launch Date Revision

Team,

I need to share an important update about our InsightEngine launch timeline.

Original Plan:
We had planned to launch InsightEngine in November 2022, as communicated in our Q3 all-hands meeting.

Revised Timeline:
After careful consideration and input from our engineering and product teams, we have decided to delay the InsightEngine launch to January 10, 2023.

This is approximately a 6-week delay from our original target.

Reasons for Delay:
1. Quality concerns: Our beta testing revealed performance issues under high load
2. Feature completeness: Key enterprise features need more development time
3. Security review: Additional security audit required for SOC 2 compliance
4. Customer feedback: Beta customers requested specific enhancements

I know this is disappointing, but I firmly believe this is the right decision. Launching a product that isn't ready would damage our reputation and customer trust far more than a delay.

Impact:
- Q4 2022 revenue projections will be revised downward
- Marketing campaigns will be rescheduled
- Customer communications will be sent this week

What This Means for You:
- Engineering: Continue focused development through the holidays
- Sales: Manage customer expectations; revised collateral coming
- Marketing: Prepare for January 10 launch campaign

I want to thank everyone for their hard work. The extra time will ensure InsightEngine launches successfully and meets the high standards our customers expect.

Please reach out if you have questions.

Best,
Sarah

---
Note: This memo supersedes previous communications about the November 2022 launch date.
