TechVenture Customer Case Study: GlobalTech Corp

Customer: GlobalTech Corp
Industry: Technology Manufacturing
Company Size: 5,000 employees
Products Used: ProjectFlow Enterprise, InsightEngine

Background:
GlobalTech Corp is a leading technology manufacturing company based in Chicago, with operations in 12 countries. Prior to implementing TechVenture solutions, they struggled with fragmented project management tools across departments.

Challenge:
- 15+ different project management tools in use across departments
- No visibility into cross-functional project status
- Frequent project delays and cost overruns
- Manual reporting consuming 20+ hours per week

Solution:
GlobalTech implemented ProjectFlow Enterprise in March 2022, followed by InsightEngine in April 2023.

Implementation Timeline:
- Phase 1 (Q1 2022): Pilot with IT department (200 users)
- Phase 2 (Q2 2022): Rollout to Engineering (800 users)
- Phase 3 (Q3 2022): Company-wide deployment (3,500 users)
- Phase 4 (Q2 2023): InsightEngine integration

Results (After 18 months):

Quantitative Improvements:
- 35% reduction in project overruns
- 25% faster project completion times
- 40% reduction in status meeting time
- $2.1M annual savings from consolidated tools
- 15 hours/week saved on manual reporting

Qualitative Improvements:
- Improved cross-department collaboration
- Real-time visibility for executives
- Better resource utilization
- Increased employee satisfaction
