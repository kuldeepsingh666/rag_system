TechVenture Customer Case Study: Retail Solutions Inc.

Customer: Retail Solutions Inc.
Industry: Retail Technology
Company Size: 2,500 employees
Products Used: ProjectFlow Business, TeamConnect

Background:
Retail Solutions Inc. provides point-of-sale and inventory management systems to retail businesses across North America. They implemented ProjectFlow in 2021 to manage their software development projects.

Challenge:
- Siloed development teams across 4 locations
- Inconsistent project management practices
- Poor visibility into project dependencies
- Customer complaints about delayed feature releases

Solution:
Retail Solutions deployed ProjectFlow Business tier in Q2 2021, followed by TeamConnect in Q4 2021.

Implementation Details:
- Users: 1,200 active users
- Projects: 45+ active software development projects
- Integration: Jira, GitHub, Confluence

Results (After 12 months):

Improvements Achieved:
- 20% reduction in project delays
- 30% improvement in cross-team collaboration
- Standardized project management across all locations
- Better resource allocation

Customer Feedback:

"ProjectFlow helped us standardize our development processes. The Jira integration was essential for our engineering teams."
- Maria Santos, CIO, Retail Solutions Inc.

"The natural language query feature is game-changing. Our non-technical managers can now access insights without IT support."
- Maria Santos, CIO, Retail Solutions Inc.

Contract Details:
- Annual Contract Value: $180,000
- Contract Term: 3 years
- Renewal Status: Renewed in 2024
