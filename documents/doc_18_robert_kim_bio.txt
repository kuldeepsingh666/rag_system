Robert Kim - Executive Biography

Name: Robert Kim
Title: VP of Data Products
Company: TechVenture Inc.

Professional Background:
Robert Kim joined TechVenture in September 2021 as VP of Data Products following TechVenture's acquisition of DataSync Solutions, which he co-founded. He reports directly to CTO Michael Rodriguez.

DataSync Solutions (Founder Journey):
Robert co-founded DataSync Solutions in 2017 with Lisa Zhang in Austin, Texas. Starting with just 3 engineers, they built the company to 45 employees before the acquisition.

Key achievements at DataSync:
- Developed proprietary real-time sync algorithms
- Grew to 120 enterprise customers
- Achieved $3.2M annual revenue
- Raised $2M in seed funding

Prior Experience:
Before founding DataSync, Robert worked at:

- 2013-2017: Senior Engineer, Oracle
  - Led data replication team
  - Developed enterprise ETL solutions
  - 15+ patents in data synchronization

- 2009-2013: Software Engineer, IBM
  - Worked on Db2 database team
  - Specialized in distributed systems

Education:
- MS Computer Science, UT Austin, 2009
- BS Computer Science, UCLA, 2007

Current Responsibilities at TechVenture:
- Oversee DataSync product line
- Lead integration of data products with InsightEngine
- Manage team of 35 engineers
- Drive data platform strategy

Key Projects Since Joining:
1. DataSync 2.0 release (50% performance improvement)
2. Integration with ProjectFlow (real-time project data sync)
3. InsightEngine data connectors (200+ sources)

Personal:
Robert lives in Austin, Texas with his family. He is active in the Austin tech community and mentors startups through Capital Factory.

Publications and Patents:
